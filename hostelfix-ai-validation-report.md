# Prototype & Validation Report — HostelFix AI

## 1. Problem Statement
Hostel maintenance complaints (plumbing, electrical, general repairs) get lost in the gap between students and the right staff member — reported informally, forgotten, or routed to the wrong person, leading to delays and repeated follow-ups. Students need a single place to report an issue once and have it reliably reach the right hands, without having to chase it up themselves.

## 2. The Prototype
**HostelFix AI** is a working digital prototype with:
- **Separate Student and Admin/Staff sign-in flows**, with demo credentials pre-filled for easy testing.
- **AI-assisted routing:** complaints are automatically directed to the appropriate staff member/department rather than requiring the student to know who to contact.
- Positioning: "Report it once. AI routes it to the right hands."

Fidelity: fully functional, login-gated prototype (built on Lovable), testable end-to-end by real users.

## 3. Testing Method
- **Testers:** 3 new testers
- **Method:** sent the live prototype link, asked each person to try the sign-in flow and share their reaction.

## 4. Tester Feedback

| Tester | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Tester 1 | "Nice idea. Hostel complaints usually get lost between people, so having one place to report and track them makes sense. The login was simple and the separate Student and Admin options are clear." |
| Tester 2 | "The concept of AI sending each complaint to the right person could save a lot of back-and-forth. The interface is clean and easy to understand at first glance. I'd like to see how it handles urgent issues like water or electrical problems." |
| Tester 3 | "Looks neat and simple, and it's clear what the app is for as soon as you open it. Having demo credentials ready is a nice touch for trying it out. It would be even better with a quick preview of what happens after you log in." |

## 5. Key Themes from Feedback
All three testers immediately understood the problem and value proposition without explanation — the "report once, AI routes it" framing landed clearly, and the student/admin split was seen as sensible. Two testers independently asked to see more of what happens *after* login: Tester 2 wanted to see urgency handling for critical issues (water/electrical), and Tester 3 wanted a preview of the post-login experience before committing to sign in. This points to the same gap — the value of the AI routing is asserted on the landing page but not yet visible in the flow testers could access.

## 6. Changes Made / Planned Based on Feedback
- Add a lightweight preview or demo walkthrough of the post-login dashboard (accessible without a full sign-in) so first-time users can see the routing/tracking in action before creating an account.
- Surface how urgency is handled for high-risk complaint types (electrical, water) more explicitly, since this was a direct, repeated ask.
