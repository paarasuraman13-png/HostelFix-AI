Complaints get lost between the student and the fixer.

In most hostels a broken fan or a leaking tap is reported by word of mouth or in a register. Nobody is sure who owns it, and the student cannot see whether anything is happening.

Today

  • Students tell a warden, a friend, or write in a register.
  • Someone has to read each complaint and decide who to call.
  • Urgent problems like sparking wires wait in the same queue as loose hinges.
  • Students have no way to check progress, so they ask again.
  • Admins cannot see which block or issue keeps repeating.

With HostelFix AI

  • Students write the problem once, in their own words.
  • AI reads it and picks the category and the right staff team.
  • Safety issues are flagged as urgent and the warden is alerted.
  • Every complaint has a status the student can check anytime.
  • Admins see all complaints in one place and spot repeat issues.

From a sentence to a fixed problem in four steps.

The student does one thing. The rest happens without anyone sorting through messages.

  1. Student describes the issue

    Sign in with a college email or student ID and write what is wrong, the way you would tell a friend.

  2. AI reads and sorts it

    It works out the category (electrical, plumbing, network and so on), how urgent it is, and the room or block if you mentioned one.

  3. The right team gets it

    The complaint goes straight to the matching staff member, so no one has to forward it by hand.

  4. Student tracks it to closure

    The status updates as staff work on it, and the admin can review what was fixed and how long it took.
